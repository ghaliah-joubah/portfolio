Kutahya – Car Sales, Rental & Repair Platform

Kutahya is a comprehensive online platform that simplifies buying, selling, renting, and repairing vehicles. Explore a wide range of vehicle options, connect with top spare parts vendors, and get the best deals on services and products. Whether you're looking to share your offers with the community or need assistance with transporting a car across cities, Kutahya is your one-stop shop for all things automotive.

Project information

  • Technologies: Vue.js, Nuxt.js, HTML, CSS (Responsive Design), JavaScript, Bootstrap, Vue 3 Carousel, i18n
  • Project URL: https://ghaliah-joubah.github.io/kutahya/
